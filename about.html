About

MY is Manisha Yadav. The musings are mine.

In short

I notice things. Then I can’t stop thinking about them until they’re written down.

I studied physics, spent three years in sales at Hindustan Unilever, and now lead a team of eleven at Samagra, working with the Government of Odisha on its programmes.

What I write here is personal: observations, opinions, and the systems I use to run my own days. I build small tools for the same reason, and a few are here if you’d like to use them.

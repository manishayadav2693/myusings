Where I'm rooted

I work on public systems in Odisha, helping government programmes reach the people they are meant for. Before that I worked in sales at Hindustan Unilever, studied at IIM Ahmedabad, and began in physics at Gargi College.

I notice the world in colours first. This is where those colours turn into words, and where I keep the small tools I build to make ordinary days run a little better.
